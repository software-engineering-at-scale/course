# Governance

Software Engineering at Scale is currently maintained by Eric Billingsley (`@ebilling`).

## Contributions

Anyone may propose changes through GitHub issues and pull requests. Contributors should use a fork and a focused branch, follow the contribution guide, and provide evidence appropriate to the change.

## Review

`@ebilling` is the sole reviewer and merger during the project's initial stage. The maintainer decides whether a change advances the course's educational purpose, preserves its principles, and meets its Definition of Done.

Approval is not based on agreement alone. A proposed change should be clear about its intended learning outcome, tradeoffs, evidence, and effect on students, instructors, and related repositories.

## Decisions

Significant decisions should be visible in an issue, pull request, or project document. The maintainer may request an experiment or a narrower proposal before accepting a consequential change.

Governance may expand as sustained contributors emerge. Any change to reviewer or merge authority will be documented here before it takes effect.
